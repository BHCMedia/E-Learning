# Chapter 2 Storyboard — What is Dementia?

## Status

Structure approved.

Detailed scene timing and narration remain pending until the full Chapter 2 script is approved.

## Target

- suggested duration: 2–3 minutes
- canvas: 1920 × 1080
- frame rate: 30 fps
- no logos
- no avatar
- no filmed footage
- no stock footage

## Proposed sequence

### Scene 1 — Umbrella term

Key idea:

Dementia is not one single condition.

Visual direction:

- one large umbrella-like conceptual structure
- several connected forms underneath it
- title: `WHAT IS DEMENTIA?`
- supporting statement: `An umbrella term for several progressive diseases`

### Scene 2 — What dementia can affect

Key concepts:

- memory
- cognitive abilities
- behaviour

Visual direction:

- person-centred central figure
- three animated concept zones
- no distressed or clinical imagery

### Scene 3 — Six course categories

Introduce:

1. Alzheimer’s disease
2. vascular dementia
3. Lewy body dementia
4. frontotemporal dementia
5. mixed dementia
6. early-onset and late-onset dementia

Visual direction:

- six distinct but visually related panels
- reveal one category at a time
- use short, approved explanations only
- avoid diagnostic oversimplification

### Scene 4 — Similar term, different experiences

Key statement:

`NO TWO PEOPLE EXPERIENCE DEMENTIA IN EXACTLY THE SAME WAY`

Visual direction:

- varied abstract individual profiles
- respectful person-centred imagery
- focus on individual experience rather than labels

### Scene 5 — Transition

On-screen copy:

- `NEXT: SYMPTOMS OF DEMENTIA`

Motion:

- gentle ambient movement
- no frozen final frame
