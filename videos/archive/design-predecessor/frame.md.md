# Healthcare eLearning Video Design System

## Design Foundation

Use the **HyperFrames Coral design** as the visual foundation.

Preserve the Coral style's:

- editorial layout system
- bold uppercase display typography
- rounded content panels
- generous spacing
- strong visual hierarchy
- clean image-and-text compositions
- simple geometric accents
- restrained, smooth motion

Do **not** use the original coral colour palette. Replace it with the healthcare learning palette defined below.

The overall result should feel:

- warm
- calm
- professional
- human
- respectful
- modern
- easy to understand
- appropriate for aged care and home care education

---

## Format

- Primary canvas: **1920 × 1080**
- Aspect ratio: **16:9**
- Frame rate: **30 fps**
- Safe margin: minimum **96 px** from all edges
- Design for clear viewing on desktop, tablet and mobile screens
- Keep essential text and graphics within the central safe area
- Avoid placing important information near the bottom edge where player controls may appear

## Colour Palette

### Primary colours

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary navy | Deep, confident healthcare blue | `#163A5F` |
| Accent teal | Calm and supportive highlight | `#2A9D8F` |
| White | Main clean background | `#FFFFFF` |
| Light grey | Secondary background and panels | `#F4F6F8` |
| Dark text | Main body copy | `#1F2A37` |

### Supporting colours

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Soft navy tint | Subtle navy background | `#E8EEF4` |
| Soft teal tint | Subtle teal background | `#E5F4F1` |
| Mid grey | Secondary text | `#667085` |
| Border grey | Dividers and card borders | `#D7DEE5` |
| Success | Correct answers and completion | `#2E7D65` |
| Warning | Important caution only | `#B7791F` |
| Error | Safety alert or incorrect response only | `#B54747` |

### Colour usage

- Use **white** or **light grey** for most scene backgrounds.
- Use **navy** for major title screens, chapter dividers and primary headings.
- Use **teal** for highlights, icons, ticks, progress markers and key words.
- Use navy and teal as accents rather than filling every surface.
- Use dark text on light backgrounds.
- Use white text only on navy or another sufficiently dark background.
- Maintain strong contrast for captions and small text.
- Never use colour alone to communicate meaning.

## Typography

Keep the typography used by the Coral design.

### Display font

**Bebas Neue**

Use for:

- video titles
- chapter titles
- major statements
- large key words
- section numbers

Rules:

- uppercase
- strong but not aggressive
- short phrases only
- generous letter spacing where needed
- avoid using it for paragraphs or detailed instructions

### Reading font

**Inter**

Use for:

- body text
- learning outcomes
- captions
- labels
- explanatory text
- quiz or scenario instructions
- supporting information

### Suggested type scale for 1920 × 1080

| Element | Font | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Main title | Bebas Neue | 110–150 px |
| Chapter title | Bebas Neue | 88–120 px |
| Key statement | Bebas Neue | 72–96 px |
| Section heading | Inter SemiBold | 46–58 px |
| Card heading | Inter SemiBold | 36–44 px |
| Body text | Inter Regular | 30–38 px |
| Captions | Inter Medium | 30–36 px |
| Small label | Inter Medium | 24–28 px |

### Typography rules

- Keep on-screen text concise.
- Prefer one clear idea per screen.
- Use no more than two font families.
- Use sentence case for Inter headings and body copy.
- Avoid long paragraphs.
- Keep most text blocks to a maximum of three short lines.
- Do not display the complete voice-over as body text.
- Emphasise only the words the learner needs to remember.

## Layout System

Retain the Coral template's editorial composition style.

### Core layout patterns

1. **Large title with offset visual**
   - Large Bebas Neue heading
   - Image, footage or graphic panel positioned beside or behind it
   - Teal line, block or marker used as an accent

2. **Split-screen learning scene**
   - Text on one side
   - Supporting footage, illustration or icon grouping on the other
   - Maintain generous negative space

3. **Rounded information cards**
   - White cards on light grey backgrounds
   - Soft border
   - Moderate corner radius
   - Minimal or no shadow
   - Teal icon or numbered marker

4. **Full-screen statement**
   - Navy background
   - Large white Bebas Neue statement
   - Small teal accent
   - Used sparingly for major learning points

5. **Checklist or learning outcomes**
   - One item enters at a time
   - Teal tick icon
   - Navy heading
   - Dark body text
   - Clear vertical rhythm

6. **Process or framework**
   - Use a left-to-right or top-to-bottom sequence
   - Distinct labelled steps
   - Teal connectors or progress markers
   - Keep the visual path simple

7. **Scenario layout**
   - Realistic footage or illustration
   - Short context statement
   - Clear “What to notice” or “How to respond” panel
   - Avoid overly dramatic visual treatment

---

## Cards and Panels

- Corner radius: **24–32 px**
- Border: **1–2 px** using `#D7DEE5`
- Card background: white
- Alternate card background: soft teal tint or soft navy tint
- Internal padding: **36–52 px**
- Keep card shadows subtle or omit them
- Avoid glossy effects, gradients and heavy drop shadows
- Do not overcrowd cards
- Use one purpose per card

---

## Images and Video Footage

Use respectful, authentic and person-centred aged care imagery.

### Preferred imagery

- staff communicating calmly with an older person
- home care visits
- residential aged care settings
- supportive everyday activities
- close-ups of respectful interaction
- familiar environments
- hands, expressions and natural human moments
- diverse workers and clients

### Image treatment

- use rounded rectangular crops
- preserve natural skin tones
- avoid cold blue filters
- use soft, natural lighting
- allow images to occupy meaningful space
- use subtle navy or teal overlays only when text requires contrast

### Avoid

- distressing or sensational images
- images that portray people living with dementia as helpless
- exaggerated confusion or aggression
- staged hospital imagery unless clinically relevant
- decorative stock footage that does not support the narration
- fast montages

---

## Iconography

- Use simple line or solid icons
- Use consistent stroke width
- Use navy for primary icons
- Use teal for highlighted or active icons
- Place icons inside soft tinted circles or rounded squares when helpful
- Avoid cartoon-like or overly playful icons
- Use icons to reinforce meaning, not replace essential explanations

Suggested icon subjects:

- memory
- communication
- behaviour
- environment
- routine
- reassurance
- safety
- observation
- support
- person-centred care

---

## Motion Style

Keep the Coral layout's confident editorial motion, but slow it slightly for learning content.

### Motion principles

- calm
- deliberate
- smooth
- predictable
- easy to follow
- never distracting

### Recommended animation

- soft fade and vertical rise
- gentle horizontal slide
- mask reveal for titles
- sequential checklist entry
- subtle image scale from 100% to 104%
- simple line or shape expansion
- progressive highlighting of key words
- step-by-step process builds

### Timing

- standard entrance: **0.5–0.8 seconds**
- major title entrance: **0.8–1.2 seconds**
- transition between scenes: **0.6–1.0 seconds**
- stagger between list items: **0.25–0.45 seconds**
- allow adequate dwell time after each important point
- synchronise entrances with the narration
- do not animate unrelated elements at the same time

### Avoid

- bouncing
- spinning
- rapid zooms
- glitch effects
- flashing
- excessive parallax
- fast kinetic typography
- constant background movement

---

## Transitions

Use a small set of repeatable transitions:

1. clean crossfade
2. navy or teal panel wipe
3. masked image reveal
4. soft vertical slide
5. chapter number transition

Use no more than two transition styles within one chapter.

---

## Captions

- Use captions for all spoken narration.
- Font: Inter Medium
- Suggested size: 32–36 px
- Maximum: two lines
- Position: lower central safe area
- Use a dark navy caption panel with white text, or dark text on a white panel
- Add comfortable horizontal and vertical padding
- Synchronise captions accurately with the narration
- Highlighting individual spoken words is optional and should be used sparingly

---

## Branding


### Chapter identification

Use a consistent chapter marker such as:

`CHAPTER 01`

Set in Bebas Neue or Inter Medium, accompanied by a short teal rule or dot.

### Progress indicator

A subtle progress indicator may be used:

- thin line
- small chapter dots
- “1 of 8”
- short navy track with a teal active section

Do not let progress indicators compete with the learning content.

---

## Audio Direction

- Narration is the primary audio element.
- Use a calm, warm and confident voice.
- Keep background music very low.
- Music is most appropriate during the opening, chapter transitions and closing.
- Reduce or remove music during detailed explanations.
- Use subtle sound effects only for purposeful moments such as:
  - title reveal
  - checklist completion
  - chapter transition
- Avoid notification-style sounds or anything that may feel playful or distracting.

---

## Accessibility and Learning Rules

- Maintain strong text contrast.
- Do not rely on colour alone.
- Give learners enough time to read every item.
- Use plain language on screen.
- Keep one learning objective or concept per scene.
- Pair narration with meaningful visuals rather than duplicate paragraphs.
- Avoid visual overload.
- Use captions throughout.
- Do not use flashing content.
- Keep essential body text at least 30 px on a 1920 × 1080 frame.
- Depict people living with dementia respectfully and as individuals.
- Use person-centred language.
- Avoid infantilising visuals, wording or sound.

## Standard Scene Templates

### 1. Course opening

- navy or white background
- large Bebas Neue course title
- short Inter subtitle
- teal accent
- calm aged care footage or image panel

### 2. Chapter opening

- chapter number
- chapter title
- one-line purpose statement
- simple navy-to-white or panel-wipe transition

### 3. Explanation scene

- short heading
- one to three key points
- one supporting visual
- key term highlighted in teal

### 4. Learning outcomes

- “Learning Outcomes” heading
- outcomes appear sequentially
- teal tick icons
- white cards or clean vertical list

### 5. Key reminder

- navy background
- one short white statement
- teal accent word or line
- longer dwell time

### 6. Scenario

- realistic situation
- concise narration
- visual focus on communication or behaviour
- response guidance appears after the situation is established

### 7. Chapter recap

- three to five takeaways
- numbered or ticked list
- light grey or white background
- teal completion marker

### 8. Closing

- short summary
- “Chapter complete” or “Continue to the next chapter”
- navy background with restrained teal accent

   - “DEMENTIA CARE”
   - Subtitle: “Understanding, recognising and responding”

## Final Creative Rule

The video must look like the **Coral HyperFrames design adapted for professional healthcare learning**:

- preserve the Coral layout logic
- preserve Bebas Neue and Inter
- preserve its strong editorial hierarchy
- preserve rounded panels and confident spacing
- replace coral with navy and teal
- keep the learning experience calm, respectful and highly readable
