Dementia is not a single condition. It is a term used for several progressive diseases that affect a person’s memory, cognitive abilities and behaviour.

Dementia can present through a range of cognitive, psychological and behavioural symptoms. These symptoms may affect communication, problem-solving, memory, coordination, judgement and everyday activities.

No two people experience dementia in exactly the same way. Understanding the individual person and how dementia affects them is an important part of providing respectful and effective support.
