# Dementia Care — Final Chapter Structure

1. **Introduction**  
   Module purpose, target audience and five learning outcomes.

2. **What is Dementia?**  
   Dementia as an umbrella term and an overview of the six common types: Alzheimer’s, vascular, Lewy body, frontotemporal, mixed, and early-/late-onset dementia.

3. **Symptoms of Dementia**  
   Cognitive, psychological and behavioural symptoms.

4. **How Dementia Presents: Behavioural**  
   Brain changes, environmental factors, health, medication and the difficulty of identifying exact causes.

5. **Managing Behavioural and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia (BPSD)**  
   Why BPSD occurs, why it is not necessarily intentional or personal, and why support must be individualised.

6. **Approaching BPSD**  
   Calm environment, routine, consistency, patience and non-confrontational communication.

7. **Managing Specific Behaviours**  
   Restlessness and agitation, aggression, and hallucinations and delusions.

8. **The ABC Approach to Behaviour Management**  
   Antecedent, behaviour, consequences, debrief and decide.

9. **Strategies for Communicating with Someone Who Has Dementia**  
   Communication, environment, activities, movement and sensory stimulation.

10. **Conclusion**  
    Compassion, patience and tailored person-centred care.
